# Data Generation Summary

## Dataset Overview

- Period: January 2022 – December 2026
- Financial institutions: 4
- Customers: 10,000
- Investment accounts: 6,500
- Loan accounts: 3,500
- Investment snapshots: 181,854

## Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Faker
- NumPy
- UUID
- PostgreSQL

## Data Characteristics

- Synthetic financial data
- Monthly investment snapshots
- Seasonal marketing expenses
- Simulated market trends
- Loan repayment and status history
- Customer satisfaction ratings (0–10)

All records are simulated and do not represent actual financial institutions or customers.# Data Generation Summary
